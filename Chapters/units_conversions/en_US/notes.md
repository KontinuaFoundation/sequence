# Units and Conversions — Notes

## Summary
Gives a reference table of common equivalences (distance, volume, mass, force, pressure, energy) and metric prefixes with a mnemonic. It teaches conversion factors and unit cancellation, applies them to light-years, kWh, and a leaking boat, explains why Celsius and Fahrenheit cannot be converted by factors, and introduces SI base units and derived units.

## Strengths
- The "fraction equals 1" method for conversion factors is clear and well illustrated with cancellation.
- Practical, relatable examples (a bug's speed, a leaking boat) make the method concrete.
- Notes when conversion factors do not apply (affine temperature scales).

## Weaknesses
- **Wrong equivalence:** "1 ounce = 0.4535924 grams" should be about 28.35 grams. 0.4535924 kg is one pound.
- **Wrong answer:** 4.5 kWh = 1.62 × 10⁷ J ÷ 4.184 ≈ 3.87 × 10⁶ calories, not 1.08 × 10⁶.
- **Typo in prefix table:** "Centi (1 × 0^{-2})" should be 10^{-2}.
- **Grammar:** "1 miles," "there has a set of units," "a really handy trick."
- **Unclear:** the mnemonic line mixes prefix names and powers; the metric prefix table skips hecto, deca, deci, and centi, but the mnemonic includes them.
- **Cleanup:** an unused ADD comment about SI constants; "Electric Current" and other base units are listed without examples.
