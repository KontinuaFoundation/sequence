# Units and Conversions — Notes

## Summary
Introduces units and how to convert between them. It gives tables of common equivalents and metric prefixes, shows the conversion-factor method with unit cancellation, applies it to a rate, explains why temperatures cannot be converted with a simple factor, and introduces SI base and derived units.
