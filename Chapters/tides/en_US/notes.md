# Tides and Eclipses — Notes

## Summary
Connects Earth's orbits to calendar rules (leap years), moon phases, lunar and solar eclipses, tidal locking, and tides. Ends with six worked exercises computing gravity, centripetal force, and lunar tidal forces on a 1 kg mass.

## Strengths
- The leap-year rules and averages (365.25, 365.2425) are correct.
- The lunar overhead/underfoot forces (≈3.43 × 10⁻⁵ N and ≈3.21 × 10⁻⁵ N) are correct.
- The barycenter and tidal-bulge argument is clearly organized, and the balance-point result (r ≈ 4,673 km) checks out.
