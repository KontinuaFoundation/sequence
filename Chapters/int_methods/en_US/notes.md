# Methods of Integration — Notes

## Summary
Covers u-substitution (a worked example and a definite-integral example), partial fractions (linear and irreducible quadratic factors, with exercises), powers of sine and cosine (odd and even), powers of secant and tangent, and integration by parts (with the choice of f and g', a definite example, and an arcsin example). Ends with exercises at each stage.
