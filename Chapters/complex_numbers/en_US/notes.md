# Complex Numbers — Notes

## Summary
Defines complex numbers a + bi with i² = −1, distinguishes imaginary numbers, gives motivations (square roots of negatives, the fundamental theorem of algebra, engineering), and covers addition and multiplication with the formula (ac − bd) + (ad + bc)i.

## Strengths
- Clear definitions, with the subset relation between imaginary and complex numbers stated plainly.
- Multiplication is shown step by step, with the i² = −1 replacement called out.
- The motivation section gives students a reason to care before the algebra.

## Weaknesses
- **Chapter is truncated:** the text ends after multiplication. Division, conjugates, the complex plane, and polar form are not covered, although the introduction promises a plane.
- **Wording:** "the imaginary part is bi" should say the imaginary part is b (a real number), with bi as the imaginary term.
- **Unsourced:** the "physics and engineering" paragraph lists uses with no example.
- **No exercises:** the chapter has no practice problems.
- **Vague reference:** "See the Khan academy video in the digital resources" with no title or link.
