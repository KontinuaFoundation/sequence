# Mechanical Advantage, Gears, and Hydraulics — Notes

## Summary
Explains mechanical advantage (output force over input force), what it does and does not change about work, how gears trade torque for speed, and how hydraulic systems multiply force. It has a worked gear-train example and exercises with answers for gears and hydraulics.

## Changes in this pass
- Corrected the gear torque formula. The driven gear's torque is T_L = (N_L / N_A) T_A. The old formula had the ratio inverted.
- Changed the addendum and dedendum units from meters to module (the module is a size unit for gear teeth).
- Fixed "visa versa" to "vice versa."
- Added a worked gear-train example (torque 30 N·m, speed one third).
- Removed contractions from the prose.
