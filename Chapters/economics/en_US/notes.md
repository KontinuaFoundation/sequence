# Basic Economics — Notes

## Summary
Introduces specialization and trade, money, profit, supply and demand, competition, regulation, time value of money, stocks and speculation, taxes, tariffs (with a worked comparison of no, low, and high tariffs), the velocity of money, the money supply, labor versus capital, and a closing section on how capitalism works.

## Strengths
- Each concept is tied to a concrete example.
- The tariff section compares three cases side by side.

## Weaknesses
- The chapter is broad and has no exercises, so readers cannot check their understanding.
- It makes contested claims (for example, on libertarianism versus socialism and on regulation) without presenting the other side.
