# Special Relativity: Summary

## What the chapter covers
Starts from the puzzle of Maxwell's speed of light and Einstein's two postulates, then derives time dilation Δt = γΔt₀ with a light clock and the Pythagorean theorem (TikZ figure). Includes a table of γ from highway speeds to 0.999c, the muon evidence (about 10% survive instead of 10⁻¹⁰) with a Python listing, length contraction from the muon's view, the twin paradox with a trip to Proxima Centauri, GPS (special and general relativity; 38 μs/day, about 11 km/day of error), and the cosmic speed limit with E = mc².

## Key ideas
- Postulates: the same laws for all steady observers; the same speed of light for everyone.
- γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²) ≥ 1; moving clocks run slow: Δt = γΔt₀.
- Lengths along the motion contract: L = L₀/γ.
- In the twin paradox, the traveler who turns around ages less.
- Nothing with mass reaches c; at rest, E = mc².
