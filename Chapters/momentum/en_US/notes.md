# Momentum — Notes

## Summary
Defines momentum (p = mv) and conservation of momentum, then contrasts elastic and inelastic collisions. It derives the elastic-collision velocity formulas from momentum and energy, covers impulse (J = FΔt) with a golf-swing example, and ends with explosions and the center-of-mass motion, including a three-fragment projectile problem.

## Strengths
- The putty opening motivates momentum and energy loss immediately.
- The billiard exercise is a full two-dimensional momentum calculation with a clean answer.
- The explosion section and three-fragment problem show how center-of-mass reasoning simplifies work.
