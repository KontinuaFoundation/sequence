# Calculus with Polar Coordinates — Notes

## Summary
Covers polar tangent slopes, the area formula ½∫r² dθ, and examples with a rose, regions between curves, and a trefoil.

## Strengths
- The cardioid tangent (slope 0 at θ = π/2) and the AP tangent (slope 1/2 at θ = 0) are correct.
- The tangent-line answers for (2/3)cos θ (slope −√3/3, point (1/2, √3/6)), 1/(2θ) (slope 2/π, line y = 2x/π + 1/π), and 2 + 3cos θ (slope −5/√3) are correct.
- The rose (9π/2), the AP trefoil (3π), polar4 item (3) (41π/4), and polar5 (≈ 0.96174) are correct.

## Weaknesses
- **Wrong answer for polar4 (1):** the integral is written without squaring, and the result is ½(e^{π/2} − e^{π/4}) ≈ 1.309, not e^{π/8}(e^{π/8} − 1) ≈ 0.712. The answer for item (2) is then the value for item (1), and item (2) (r = 2 sin θ + cos 2θ) is never answered.
- **Mislabeled derivative:** "dr/dθ = (dy/dθ)/(dx/dθ)" should be dy/dx.
- **Wrong slope:** polar3 (3) states −√5/3 where −5/√3 is correct.
- **Typo:** "Figure-\ref" and an unresolved FIXME about polar arc length.
