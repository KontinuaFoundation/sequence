# Rational Functions: Summary

## What the chapter covers
Rational expressions and functions: simplifying, domains, asymptotes, and partial fraction decomposition.

## Key ideas
- A rational function is one polynomial divided by another.
- Factoring reveals holes, vertical asymptotes, and horizontal asymptotes.
- Partial fractions break a rational function into simpler pieces.
