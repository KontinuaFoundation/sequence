# Triple Integrals — Notes

## Summary
Extends double integrals to solids E: iterated triple integrals with constant and variable limits, cylindrical coordinates (dV = r dz dr dθ), and spherical coordinates (dV = ρ² sin φ dρ dφ dθ). It ends with a rule for choosing a coordinate system and four exercises (box integral 3/2, cylinder volume 4π, ball volume 32π/3, and an iterated integral for the octant tetrahedron, 1/6).
