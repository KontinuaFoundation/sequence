# Gases and Heat — Notes

## Summary
Covers molecular speeds and temperature, Kelvin, Charles's and Boyle's laws, the ideal gas law (with an O₂ cylinder example), molar heat capacities (Cv = 3R/2, Cp = 5R/2), work and the 499 J difference, adiabatic compression with a Python simulation and the γ rules, an air-conditioner model, specific heat and thermal equilibrium, phases and latent heat, thermal-energy-storage exercises (water and mirabilite), and evaporation and humidity.
