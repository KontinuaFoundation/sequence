# Diodes, Rectifiers, and Photovoltaics — Notes

## Summary
Introduces silicon and covalent bonding, diodes (current in one direction and the 0.7 V drop), half-wave and full-wave rectifiers that turn AC into DC, and photovoltaic (solar) cells. It has a worked current example and an exercise with an answer.

## Changes in this pass
- Corrected the photovoltaics explanation: the junction makes a 0.7 V barrier (not an electrical field), the electron cannot cross back over the barrier, and the charge carriers are a hole and an electron, not a proton.
- Fixed "Sometimes is it excited" to "Sometimes it is excited."
- Added a worked example (5 V, 1 kOhm, 0.7 V drop gives 4.3 mA) and an exercise (12 V, 2 kOhm gives 5.65 mA). Both values were checked.
