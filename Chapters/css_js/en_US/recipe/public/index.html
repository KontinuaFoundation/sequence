Chocolate Chip Cookies

This recipe makes soft and sweet chocolate chip cookies.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in one bowl.
  3. Mix the butter, sugar, brown sugar, and eggs in another bowl.
  4. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients.
  5. Add the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes.

Recipe Facts

Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe Facts
Prep Time 15 minutes
Bake Time 10 minutes
Servings 24 cookies

Important Note: Make sure not to overbake the cookies, or they will become hard.
