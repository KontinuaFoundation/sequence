# Span — Notes

## Summary
Explains span geometrically (one vector or dependent set spans a line, two independent vectors a plane, three independent vectors R³) and formally, then tests independence for a three-vector example and a short determining-span exercise.

## Strengths
- The line/plane/R³ summary and the span exercise answers are correct (the third set spans R³ because det = −9 ≠ 0).
- The statement that dependent vectors give a line, and independent pairs a plane, matches the geometry.

## Weaknesses
- **Inconsistent vector:** the example defines t = [0, 2, 4], but the dependence check uses [0, 1, 2]. With t = [0, 2, 4] the relation is a₂ = 2.5a₁, not 5. The conclusion (s and t dependent) still holds.
- **Typo in the system:** "−6a₂ − 10a₂ = 0" should be −6a₁ − 10a₂ (the coefficient of a₁ is missing).
