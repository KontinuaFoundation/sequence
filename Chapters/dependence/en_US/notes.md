# Linear Independence and Dependence — Notes

## Summary
Defines linear independence and dependence (c₁v₁ + … + cₖvₖ = 0 only trivially), tests pairs and sets (including the zero vector), finds the k values that make vectors dependent, connects independence to span and basis, and works a geometric example.

## Strengths
- All independence and dependence answers are correct (for example, [4, −2] = 2[2, −1] and [1, −2, −3] = −½[−2, 4, 6]).
- The k-values exercise is correct (k = 2; always dependent for the second pair).
- The colinear exercise is correct (a, d, f dependent; c, e dependent; b independent).

## Weaknesses
- **Wrong statement:** "In ℝ², the vectors are generally perpendicular" is false. Independent vectors need not be perpendicular.
