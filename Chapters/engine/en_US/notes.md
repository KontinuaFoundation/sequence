# The Piston Engine — Notes

## Summary
Explains the parts of a four-stroke piston engine, the energy budget (30% to the crank, 35% exhaust, 30% coolant, 5% friction), lubrication, common problems, how engines are measured (bore, stroke, displacement, compression ratio), the Ford Model T, a fuel-mix exercise using the ideal gas law (0.001613 mol ethanol, 2.145 kJ per burn), compression ratio and octane, and carburetors and fuel injection.
