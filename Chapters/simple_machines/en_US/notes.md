# Simple Machines — Notes

## Summary
Covers inclined planes (mechanical advantage as length over height, and the force needed on a frictionless ramp), levers and the seesaw, pulleys, and wedges. It has a worked example on the inclined plane and several exercises with answers.

## Changes in this pass
- Corrected the explanation of ramp steepness: a gentler ramp has a larger mechanical advantage, not a steeper one.
- Added a worked example: a 200 N crate on a 5 m ramp that rises 1 m needs 40 N.
- Removed contractions from the prose.
