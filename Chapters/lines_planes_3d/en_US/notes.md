# Lines and Planes in Three Dimensions — Notes

## Summary
Covers points and vectors in space, lines (vector and parametric equations), planes (normal form and linear equation), the distance from a point to a plane, and where a line meets a plane. Ends with exercises on a point on a line, a plane through three points, and a distance.
