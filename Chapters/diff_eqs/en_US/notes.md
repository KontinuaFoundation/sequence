# Differential Equations — Notes

## Summary
Introduces ODEs and PDEs. Covers the spring equation (x'' = −(k/m)x), population growth (exponential and logistic models with a worked example and exercises), separable equations (with the general solution y = √(2x³/3 + K)), the AP bird-growth problem, and a linear-versus-exponential growth question.
