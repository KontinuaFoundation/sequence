# Rules for Finding Derivatives — Notes

## Summary
Covers the constant, power, product, quotient, and chain rules, then practice, applications (velocity, acceleration, tangent lines, L'Hôpital's rule, and the mean value theorem), and a conclusion. It has eighteen exercises with answers and two worked examples.
