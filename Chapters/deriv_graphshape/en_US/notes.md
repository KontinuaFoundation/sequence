# First and Second Derivatives and the Shape of a Function — Notes

## Summary
Uses the first derivative to find increasing and decreasing intervals, critical values, and local and global extrema (with a table of signs), sketches functions from their derivatives, and uses the second derivative for concavity and inflection points. It has twelve exercises with answers and one worked example.

## Changes in this pass

- Checked the critical-point and global-extrema values (f(x) = 12 + 4x - x^2 gives 12, 16, and 7 at x = 0, 2, 5), the increasing-decreasing example (f' = 300 - 3x^2 with roots ±10), and the logarithm derivative (f' = (1 - ln x)/x^2).
