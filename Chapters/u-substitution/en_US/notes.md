# u-Substitution — Notes

## Summary
Explains u-substitution as the reverse of the chain rule, works two indefinite examples, gives five indefinite exercises with answers, and derives the substitution rule for definite integrals, comparing changing limits with back-substitution on ∫₀⁵ √(3x+1) dx = 14.

## Strengths
- Every exercise answer is correct, and the answers include derivative checks.
- The definite-integral example gives the same result (14) by both methods.
- The worked √(x²−1)·x⁵ example shows how to deal with a leftover x, which students usually find hard.

## Weaknesses
- **Typo in the worked example:** ∫₀⁵ √(3x+3) dx should read √(3x+1).
- **Wording:** "How to we use" should be "How do we use."
- **No integration by parts or trig substitution content:** these are named as related techniques but not covered.
