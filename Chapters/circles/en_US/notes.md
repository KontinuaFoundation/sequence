# Circles — Notes

## Summary
Defines circles, area (πr²), circumference (πd), spheres, and arc length (rθ), derives 2π radians from the arc formula, and covers sector area (θ/2 · r²). It ends with tangent and secant lines and a "painting a comet" exercise that combines tangents, sectors, and triangles.

## Strengths
- Derives the radian and arc-length results from the definition of a radian, which is the key idea of the chapter.
- The painting-a-comet problem ties tangency and right angles to area.
- Practical exercises (tree trunk, pie, tire rotation) motivate the formulas.

## Weaknesses
- **Wrong number:** the pizza example says radius 15 cm but uses 18 cm (A = 324π should be 225π ≈ 706.9 cm²; each slice is 37.5π).
- **Typo in answer:** the sector exercise uses radius 21.5 m; it should be 2.5 m. The result 7.363 m² is correct for 2.5 m.
- **Ranking error:** arc lengths are π/2 (1), π (2), π/2 (3), 9π/4 (4), so the order is 4, 2, then (1, 3). The answer says 4, (1,3), 2.
- **Wrong sentence:** "Every chord lies on a secant line but not every secant line contains a chord." A secant always contains the chord between its two intersection points, so the second clause is false.
- **Comet answer is wrong:** the sector area is (θ/2)r² = ½(4.03)(9) = 18.14, not 36.27, and the two triangles (18.9 total) are counted twice. The total is about 37.0 m², or about 6.2 L, not 12.35 L.
