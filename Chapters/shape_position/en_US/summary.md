# Shape, Position, and Outliers: Summary

## What the chapter covers
How skew and outliers affect the mean, median, and standard deviation; the effect of changing units; z-scores; and percentiles, quartiles, and the five-number summary.

## Key ideas
- In skewed data, the mean is pulled toward the tail; the median resists outliers.
- A z-score says how many standard deviations a value is from the mean.
- Quartiles and the five-number summary describe position and spread.
