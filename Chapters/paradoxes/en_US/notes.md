# Laws, Paradoxes, Thought Experiments, and the Illusions — Notes

## Summary
A survey of paradoxes and thought experiments in mathematics (Russell's paradox, Simpson's paradox, the Monty Hall problem, the birthday problem, Zeno's paradoxes), physics (Schrödinger's cat, time travel), architecture and geometry (Penrose steps, the Möbius strip, the coastline paradox), and computer science (Moravec's paradox, neural scaling laws). It introduces proof by contradiction along the way.
