# Optimization — Notes

## Summary
Covers extrema and the first and second derivative tests, a general strategy for solving optimization problems, and a table of common optimization setups. It has a worked example of a minimum and three exercises with answers (a minimum of a quadratic, a fenced enclosure, and a Python script that finds a critical point).
