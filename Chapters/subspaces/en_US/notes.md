# Subspaces — Notes

## Summary
Defines a subspace (contains 0, closed under addition and scalar multiples), equates subspaces with spans, defines basis and dimension, then covers the nullspace, row space, and column space with worked examples and an exercise.

## Strengths
- The nullspace of [[1, 2], [2, 4]] (span of (−2, 1)) and the 4×4 RREF example's RREF are correct.
- The column-space example's basis (columns 1, 2, 4 of the original matrix) is correct.
- The big exercise's RREF, nullspace, row-space, and column-space answers are correct.

## Weaknesses
- **Wrong sign:** for the 4×4 nullspace example, the second basis vector should be (6, −3, 0, 1), not (6, −3, 0, −1).
- **Wrong terminology:** in the nullspace exercise, x₃, x₄, and x₅ are called "bound or fixed" when they are the free variables. The third basis vector also reuses x₄ where x₅ is meant.
- **Wrong terminology:** the column-space example says "there is a free row"; it should say a zero row, with a free column.
