# Subspaces — Notes

## Summary
Defines a subspace (contains 0, closed under addition and scalar multiples), equates subspaces with spans, defines basis and dimension, then covers the nullspace, row space, and column space with worked examples and an exercise. Ends with a diagram of the four fundamental subspaces.
