# Antiderivatives — Notes

## Summary
Introduces antiderivatives from a graph, the constant of integration, specific antiderivatives, trigonometric and exponential antiderivatives (including ln|x| and b^x), higher-order antiderivatives, and practice problems with answers.

## Strengths
- The graphical sketch of F from f is a good intuition builder.
- The trig table is correct, and the motion problem (s(t) = t³ + 2t² − 6t + 9) and the −2cos x − 1 solution are right.
- The exponential and higher-order examples, including f(x) = x³/3 + 3eˣ + C₁x + C₂, are correct.

## Weaknesses
- **Arithmetic error:** F(3) = (1/3)(3)² + C should read (1/3)(3)³ = 9. The line "39 + C = 2" repeats the mistake, though the final C = −7 is correct.
- **Wrong constant:** in the last exercise, f(1) = 1 gives C₁ = −3, not 3. The final answer also has −4 where the constant should be +4, since f(0) = 4.
- **Typo:** "the antiderivative of 2x is x_2" should be x².
- **Typo:** the trig working line says tan x where tan θ is needed.
- **Unit typo:** "cm/s^s" should be cm/s².
