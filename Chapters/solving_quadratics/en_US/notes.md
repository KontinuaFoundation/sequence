# Solving Quadratics — Notes

## Summary
Reduces ax² + bx + c = 0 to monic form, derives the completed-square root formula, and interprets the discriminant with parabolas. It solves the falling-hammer equation (t ≈ 2.61 s), derives the quadratic formula, and then covers factoring (GCF, trinomials with a = 1, the AC method, perfect squares, difference of squares).

## Strengths
- Parabola pictures for each discriminant case make the cases concrete.
- The hammer example returns to the earlier chapter, closing the loop.
- The AC method example (6x² + 11x + 4) is worked fully and correctly.

## Weaknesses

- **Missing:** no practice problems on factoring; the factoring section has only worked examples.
