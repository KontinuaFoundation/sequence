# Moment of Inertia and Angular Momentum: Summary

## What the chapter covers
Moment of inertia and rotational F = ma (τ = Iα), the parallel-axis theorem, rotational kinetic energy and rolling, flywheels, angular momentum, angular impulse, work done by torque, and a linear-versus-rotational summary table.

## Key ideas
- Moment of inertia I = Σmr²: mass far from the axis counts most.
- τ = Iα, and rotational kinetic energy is ½Iω².
- Angular momentum L = Iω is conserved when no outside torque acts.
